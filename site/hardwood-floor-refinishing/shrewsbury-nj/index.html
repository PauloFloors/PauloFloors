Common Questions

Hardwood Floor Refinishing FAQ

Can my floors be refinished instead of replaced?

In most cases, yes — refinishing is often a faster, more affordable way to restore hardwood.

How many times can a floor be refinished?

Solid hardwood can typically be refinished multiple times over its life, depending on how much wood remains above the tongue and groove.

Will the new finish match my existing floors?

Paulo works to match stain color as closely as possible when blending refinished sections with existing hardwood.

How long before I can walk on my floors again?

Drying time depends on the finish used — Paulo will let you know what to expect for your project.
